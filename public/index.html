THE IDEA IS SIMPLE

One request.
A few things off your mind.

  1. 01

    Start with the mess

    Write what you know about the event. It does not have to sound polished.

  2. 02

    Fill the real gaps

    Confirm the missing facts. A venue or a price should never be quietly invented.

  3. 03

    Review, then use

    Edit your drafts, resolve the questions, approve a version, and download your pack.
