## Evidence labels

- Fictional event information only. No attendee records or credentials are shown.
- Earlier successful Community Repair Day generation, update and downloaded-PDF evidence was captured on 23 October 2026 and is labelled separately from the new recording attempts.
- New recording attempts hit timeouts and Google HTTP 500. The film discloses this instead of presenting a sample as successful inference.
- The manual review controls use the hand-authored School Family Day sample. Facts and wording are edited explicitly; unknown pricing keeps the sample a draft.
- The recorded live update omitted 09:30 from the invitation; the film distinguishes the subsequent editor correction from model output.
- Camera movement, retiming and cursor emphasis are editorial additions. These are edited demonstrations, not uninterrupted screen recordings or performance benchmarks.
- Render explanation panels summarize verified service data. They are not simulated dashboard screenshots. The browser dashboard required sign-in and is not depicted.
- The Copilot contribution was Copilot Chat, evaluated before fixes. It was not a registered formal PR review.
- The background music was synthesized for the videos; no third-party commercial song is used.
