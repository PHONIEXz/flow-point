# Letter layout and requested after-event duties

The Invitation tab offers Formal letter / Informal letter and Apply letter layout. This local action preserves body text, supplies conventional salutation/subject/closing and the actual signatory, and invalidates approval. It makes no AI call. The body remains directly editable. The helper formats rather than rewrites the prose; hosted generation receives the chosen style for new wording. No postal address, recipient identity or letter issue date is invented. Formal is the default.

Generation prompts now explicitly require normal letter structure and before/on/after-event checklist coverage. Conservative source checks reject omission of explicitly requested cleanup, return of borrowed equipment, spending reconciliation and feedback from an After the event clause. Missing duties cause bounded regeneration, not silent insertion of invented owners/deadlines. Other paraphrases and checklist duties still need review. Previous generation checkpoints are invalidated by the new contract.

The interrupted v1 evaluation remains unchanged. Post-fix testing uses a separate freeze and output directory; previously executed examples are exposed regressions, not new blind evidence.
