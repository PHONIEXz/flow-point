## Initial brief

EVENT NAME AND PURPOSE

Our school, Sunrise Learning Centre, Abuja, is organising its first “Young Minds: Science, Reading and Creativity Day” on Saturday, 14 November 2026.

The programme will allow pupils to demonstrate classroom learning through simple science projects, storytelling, reading presentations and creative displays. We also want parents to understand what their children have been learning and encourage practical learning at home.

This is an educational school event, not a fundraising programme. Attendance is free. Do not include ticket sales, compulsory donations or sponsorship promises.

ATTENDANCE AND VENUE

We expect 150 people altogether: 100 pupils, 30 parents or guardians, and 20 teachers and support staff.

The proposed venue is the school’s Main Assembly Hall. Its use has not yet been confirmed. The school library is our backup venue, but its seating capacity still needs checking.

We intend to hold a three-hour programme. The exact starting time has not been approved by the headteacher. Please leave the start time unconfirmed and use relative timings or clearly labelled proposals when preparing the programme. Do not present a suggested time as an approved decision.

INVITATION DETAILS

The invitation should address parents and guardians warmly and professionally. Explain the purpose of the event, the activities they can expect and that attendance is free.

Include the event date and proposed venue, but clearly indicate that the starting time and final venue will be confirmed later. Do not invent a telephone number, email address, RSVP deadline or contact person.

The invitation should be signed by Mrs Grace Okoro, Headteacher, on behalf of Sunrise Learning Centre.

PROGRAMME CONTENT

Please organise the following activities into a practical three-hour programme:

• Welcome and housekeeping: 10 minutes.
• Opening remarks by the headteacher: 10 minutes.
• Pupils’ reading and storytelling presentations: 15 minutes.
• Science demonstrations: 35 minutes.
• Refreshment break: 15 minutes.
• Guided viewing of pupils’ project displays: 30 minutes.
• Parent and pupil reading activity: 20 minutes.
• Creativity presentations and recognition of participants: 25 minutes.
• Vote of thanks: 10 minutes.
• Closing remarks and supervised departure: 10 minutes.

Science activities should use simple, low-risk classroom materials. Avoid flames, exposed electrical connections, sharp tools or experiments involving hazardous chemicals.

Teachers should supervise demonstrations, display areas and pupil movement. Parents should remain with younger children during the display tour.

Mrs Grace Okoro, Headteacher, will approve the programme, invitation, venue and expenditure.

Mr Daniel Musa, Science Coordinator, will organise the science demonstrations and check materials.

The school’s designated first-aid officer should be available throughout the programme. The officer’s name has not been confirmed, so include this as an outstanding assignment.

PLANNING AND PREPARATION

Please create a useful checklist covering preparation before the event, responsibilities on the day and work after the event.

Before the event, we need to confirm the venue and starting time, approve the invitation, notify parents, select presentations, check demonstration safety, arrange seating, obtain outstanding quotations, prepare printed materials and rehearse the programme.

A rehearsal is proposed for Thursday, 12 November 2026, during the final school period. The rehearsal time still needs approval.

On the day, staff should check the venue, test the sound system, organise registration, position drinking water, check emergency exits and supervise pupils.

Registration should collect only the minimum information the school needs. Do not put pupil names, photographs or private family information into the public invitation.

After the event, staff should supervise collection of pupils, clean the venue, return borrowed equipment, reconcile spending and record brief feedback.

We also need one event banner and 10 recognition prizes, but their prices are not confirmed.
